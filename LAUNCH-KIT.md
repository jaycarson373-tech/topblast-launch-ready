## Stonk description

TopBlast is a launchpad layer on StonkFun and Pump.fun, built for the holders who bought the top. Verified buyers who keep holding below their average entry can qualify for rewards from their token's funded pool. Each launch has separate accounting and public payout proof. Creator deposits fund rewards; automatic creator-fee routing is not enabled. No guaranteed payouts or loss recovery. Currently in controlled testing.

## Launch thesis

Bought in. Price dropped. Still holding?

That is who TopBlast is built for.

StonkFun and Pump.fun provide the launch infrastructure. TopBlast adds the reward layer: verified buys, a real average entry and a separate funded pool for each launch.

Your entry sets the line. Holding below it can make you eligible. The funded pool sets the limit. Confirmed transactions provide the proof.

Transfers do not invent an entry price. Selling or sending tokens out excludes that wallet for the epoch. One token's funds never pay another token's holders.

Creators fund their launch's rewards through explicit deposits. This is not insurance, guaranteed recovery or an automatic share of venue fees.

The site is deployed. Controlled launch testing is the next step. Public reward activation follows a demonstrated onchain cycle, not a homepage claim.

Your token. Your venue underneath. TopBlast on top.
